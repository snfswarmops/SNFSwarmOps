The **SwarmOps** project (SNSF Grant 219732) has officially started at the University of Bern.

The project focuses on **human-sensing-based MLOps for collaborative AI-enabled Cyber-Physical Systems**, targeting applications such as UAVs, smart cities, and autonomous systems.
