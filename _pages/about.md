SwarmOps is an SNSF-funded research project focused on **AI-enabled Cyber-Physical Systems (AI-CPSs)**, with a particular emphasis on **human-sensing-based MLOps** for collaborative and safety-critical systems.

The project addresses key challenges in enabling **reliable, adaptive, and scalable multi-agent AI systems** operating in real-world environments such as autonomous drones, smart cities, and disaster response scenarios.

---

## Quick Facts

- **Grant:** SNSF Project **219732**
- **Title:** *SwarmOps — Human-sensing based MLOps for Collaborative Cyber-Physical Systems*
- **Duration:** **2025–2029**
- **Principal Investigator:** **Sebastiano Panichella**
- **Funding:** **667,280 CHF**
- **Status:** Ongoing

---

## Project Vision

SwarmOps aims to enable **collaborative teams of AI-CPSs** that can:

- Reliably operate in **dynamic and uncertain environments**
- Safely interact with **humans and other agents**
- Continuously improve through **data-driven MLOps pipelines**

---

## Key Research Directions

- **MLOps for AI-CPSs** (monitoring, deployment, continuous learning)
- **Human-sensing integration** for adaptive systems
- **Multi-agent collaboration and coordination**
- **Reliability, safety, and verification of AI systems**
