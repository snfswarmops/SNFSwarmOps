Ali Javadi is a Research Assistant (PhD candidate) at the University of Bern, working on AI-enabled Cyber-Physical Systems within the SwarmOps project.

His research focuses on **deep reinforcement learning, autonomous systems, and robustness testing of UAVs**, with particular emphasis on generating challenging environments for safety-critical AI systems.

He has experience in:
- Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL)
- Robotics and simulation (Isaac Gym, ROS)
- Computer Vision and ML systems
- MLOps and AI system evaluation

Ali has contributed to international competitions such as **ICST and SBFT UAV Testing Tracks**, focusing on benchmarking and robustness of autonomous systems.

He previously worked as a Machine Learning Engineer in industry, developing large-scale AI systems for computer vision and NLP applications.
