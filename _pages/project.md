## Overview

SwarmOps aims to redefine MLOps for **collaborative AI-enabled Cyber-Physical Systems (AI-CPSs)**.

Modern systems such as UAV swarms, autonomous vehicles, and smart infrastructure require:
- Real-time decision making
- Robust multi-agent coordination
- Safe interaction with humans and dynamic environments

## Key Challenges

- Lack of **MLOps frameworks for multi-agent AI systems**
- Limited **environment perception and adaptation**
- Poor **reliability and safety guarantees**

## Objectives

- Develop **human-sensing-based MLOps pipelines**
- Enable **safe and adaptive multi-agent collaboration**
- Improve **verification and monitoring of AI decisions**

## Application Domains

- Autonomous drones (UAVs)
- Smart cities
- Disaster response systems
- Robotics and industrial CPS
